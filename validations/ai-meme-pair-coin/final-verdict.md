## One-paragraph reason

The agent-run-fees mechanic is covered by Clanker, Agency, Metaplex and Pump.fun [S], the AI agent sector fell 75-90% [S], and the founder's own Accelerando reference token is dormant ($6.8K, zero volume) [M]. Nothing found shows that anyone wants this; nothing proves they don't. The verdict is capped at NEEDS MORE RESEARCH by the demand rule (verified demand ≤ 1), and the evidence does not justify going higher.
