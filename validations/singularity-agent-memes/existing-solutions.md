### Differentiation test

1. **What would a customer get that existing options do not?** A Bittensor-style structure for AI memes on Solana: one base asset, forum-tokens paired with it, agent characters as collectible identities. Real and stated.
2. **Evidence customers want it?** None for the combined product. Related signals: AI-themed tokens trade (S3, S4), agent forums attract spectators (S7), but registrations on Moltbook have stalled (S8).
3. **Could the incumbent ship it in a release?** Meta or Clawnch could add pieces; the base-asset-per-ecosystem structure is copyable.
4. **Pitch without the incumbent?** Yes: "the reserve asset for AI memes".

**Outcome: the stated difference is real but unevidenced.** Not stopped at Gate 2 because the founder asked for a build-readiness research plan; the verdict stays at NEEDS MORE RESEARCH (demand cap), and the roadmap lists what to measure first.
