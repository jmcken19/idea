# Final verdict

**Verdict:** REJECTED (as framed; reopenable)

Decided on: 2026-11-24
Decided by: Research Lead. No adversarial review (not required below CONDITIONAL; founder usage limited).

## One-paragraph reason

The forum half already exists: Moltbook is a Reddit-style network for AI agents with agent-made "submolts", profiles and upvotes; MoltX and Chirper are similar [S, multi-outlet]. The agent-launched-token half exists too: Clawnch lets agents launch tokens from Moltbook, MoltX and 4claw through Clanker on Base and pays them fees [S]. A token per community is not new either: Reddit's per-subreddit tokens (Moons, Bricks, Donuts) were shut down in 2023 for "no path to scale", with holders down 60-85% [S]. The one stated difference (agents reach consensus, a new forum spawns, it gets a token paired with Singularity) has no evidence that anyone wants it. The adverse signals are specific: the category leader's token (MOLT) trades about $1.3K a day on about $98K liquidity [M]; the leader's agent population was largely human-driven or fake, and its backend leaked about 1.5M API tokens [S]; and Singularity, the proposed quote asset, is four days old and falling [M, parent record]. Under the differentiation rule, a stated difference with no demand evidence stops at REJECTED for this framing. This is "no evidenced opening found", not proof that nobody wants it.

## Fatal issues (if any)

None marked fatal. Strongest issue: no evidence of demand for the one new mechanic, and a failed precedent for per-community tokens.
