# Memecoin and launchpad platform patterns (as of Nov 2026)

These patterns reliably predict how a new idea in this space will fare. Sources: `research/market-landscape/solana-launchpads-2026.md` (S/M grade) and the salary research. Re-check every 90 days.

1. **Native features absorb tools.** Pump.fun shipped fee sharing, ownership transfer, holder rewards and custom pairs during 2026. A tool built on top of one platform feature can be made redundant by the next release. (−) for any "layer on Pump.fun" idea.
2. **Flow beats features.** Traders and deployers go where liquidity, bots and terminals are. Every share gain by a challenger was temporary.
3. **Creator-payout narratives collapse.** Bags, Zora, Believe and Base creator rewards all declined 90–99%+ from their peaks.
4. **Fees are concentrated.** A few dozen coins earn most creator fees, so any product that monetises creator fees has a tiny paying segment.
5. **Trust tools are crowded and free.** Rug checkers, dev-history tools, bubble maps and terminal badges are mostly free, and they are distributed through terminals that traders already use.
6. **Accountability has buyers only in curated fundraising** (MetaDAO), not in meme launches.
