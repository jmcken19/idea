# Token necessity test

Run this for any idea that proposes a token. Judge the product without the token first. If the product fails without it, the token does not rescue it.

| # | Question | Pass condition | Kill signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What does the product do with no token? | It has a payer and a price on its own | "The token is how we make money" |
| 2 | What can a holder do with the token that a non-holder cannot? | A specific in-product right that users need (access, collateral, governance with real authority) | Only "number go up", or a discount |
| 3 | Would the product work just as well with SOL/USDC instead? | No: the design truly needs a native unit | Yes → the token is a fundraising mechanism |
| 4 | How does value accrue, and who captures the fees? | Fees accrue to holders through a mechanism that is legal where you operate | Promised revenue share or buybacks to passive holders (securities risk) |
| 5 | What stops extraction (insiders dumping)? | On-chain vesting that nobody can cancel, public allocation, no unlocked team supply | Unlocked team supply, cancellable locks (89.7% of Jupiter Locks are cancellable, see crypto-infrastructure) |
| 6 | What liquidity is needed, and who provides it? | Named source and budget | "The community" |
| 7 | Distribution risk | Concentration below a stated threshold, snipers addressed | Bundled or sniped launch (common on Pump.fun) |
| 8 | Regulatory exposure | No profit promises; utility is real and current | Any "holders earn" language |
| 9 | Do similar token models already operate, and how did they do? | Comparable tokens with data (CoinGecko, DefiLlama) | Comparables collapsed (Zora, Believe, Bags fee decline) |

Outcome: **Necessary** / **Optional (product stands alone)** / **Unnecessary (fundraising only)** / **Harmful (adds legal or extraction risk)**. Record it in `commercial-analysis.md`.

Not legal advice. Any token with value accrual needs counsel.
