# Solana and cross-chain launchpad landscape, 2026

Imported 2026-11-24 from `jmcken19/salary` research-v2/01 and /00 (written Nov 24, 2026). **Warning:** that research was compiled "from web search snippets only (most pages could not be fetched)". ## Facts worth reusing (with original confidence)
- Pump.fun made about 78% of new Solana tokens from Nov 2025 to Oct 2026 (Bitquery) [M]. Revenue about $31.8M in Jan 2026, down about 75% year on year (DefiLlama via Cointelegraph) [H; re-seen in a Nov 24 search].
- Pump.fun fees in the last 30 days: $61.98M, of which $44.95M was protocol revenue, so about $17M a month went to creators plus cashback (DefiLlama, read Nov 23 2026 in salary research) [M].
- Creator fees are concentrated: about $115M in total creator fees since May 2025, mostly to a few dozen coins (research-v2/02) [M].
- 2026 Pump.fun changes: fee sharing (Jan 9), Cashback (Feb), one-change cap (Mar 25), Holder Rewards (Sep 12). Each changes who gets the fee stream [H].
- Challengers won share only with short-lived levers (buybacks, a bigger fee share, a new chain); each lead faded within 4–8 weeks [M].
- Creator-payout platforms collapsed: Bags monthly fees $20.06M → about $186K (Jan → Oct 2026); Zora launches fell from 118,069 to 852 a day; Believe revenue near zero in 2026, plus a class action [M].
- MetaDAO (futarchy raises, team allowance) is the one builder-accountability model with strong demand signals ($25.6M over 8 sales by Jan 2026; heavy oversubscription) [M], but it is a curated fundraising venue.
- Pump.fun's co-founder said creator fees had little effect on the average deployer's behaviour (Jan 2026) [H].

## Recurring patterns (see also `research/industry-trends/`)
1. People switch launchpads for flow, liquidity and money now, not for features.
2. Incumbents copy successful features within days (custom pairs after StonkFun).
3. A higher creator fee share attracts fee farmers, not builders.
