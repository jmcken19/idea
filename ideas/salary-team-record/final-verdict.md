# Final verdict

**Verdict:** REJECTED

Decided on: 2026-11-24
Decided by: Research Lead, after the independent adversarial inverse check (rejection stands)

## One-paragraph reason

The core of the pivot, a deployer/team track record for traders, is an existing category. MadeOnSol Deployer Hunter, Mobula's dev-history and wallet-deployer APIs (covering Pump.fun, PumpSwap and Meteora-based launchpads such as Bags, Moonshot and Believe), Rug Munch, Noesis and the terminals' "dev sold" / insider / bundle signals (GMGN, DEXTools) already cover it, much of it free [S: S2–S8, S12, S13, S17]. That removes the claimed "cross-launchpad" difference. What is left (team roster, lock settings, fee-split recipients) is a few public account reads that any incumbent can add in one release [V: S9]. It has no usage or payment evidence, and most coins would show empty fields (0 of 20 sampled multi-wallet coins had team locks [V: S9]). The Team Launch half overlaps launch-time vesting (Jupiter Studio) and KYC'd creator launchpads (Doxxxed.fun, listed for sale at $75k [M: S16]). Revenue depends on the same small, fee-concentrated team segment that sank the parent idea.

## Fatal issues
- Existing equivalents plus a copyable, undemanded difference (the Salary gate: #1 ≤ 1 and #2 ≤ 1).

## What evidence would change this verdict
- A terminal or launchpad committing to show and pay for a team-record badge (level-4 demand), **and**
- measured adoption: at least 10 of 100 builder teams taking a free concierge "team launch" within 4 weeks (the parent research's own kill test).
Either result would justify a new intake, not a reopening of this one.

## Evidence quality
Competitor evidence is mostly search-snippet grade (S), because page fetches are blocked in this environment. The rejection does not depend on any single snippet: six independent tools point the same way, and the chain-data points are V. Not checked: GitHub and other chains; whether GMGN, Axiom or DexScreener token pages show a dev's past coins; Pump.fun creator profiles. Re-verify when the data hosts are allowed.
