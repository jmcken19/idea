# Stage 1: Intake

Parent: [`ideas/salary`](../salary/) (REJECTED). A pivot is a new idea (Rule R4). This is the research-v2 proposal [S1] as of 2026-11-24.

## The idea in the submitter's words (verbatim)

> Salary pivot: a public, cross-launchpad Team Record for every coin (deployer history, team roster, locks, fee recipients, payday history) plus a Team Launch flow on Pump.fun with a time-locked team multisig as creator and locked team tokens; payroll becomes a feature.

## Restated without expansion

| Element | Statement |
|---|---|
| Problem | Traders can't tell builder teams from serial deployers; builder teams can't prove they're different |
| Who has it | (a) traders choosing coins; (b) builder teams launching coins |
| Proposed solution | A free public record per coin and builder (deployer history, roster, locks, fee recipients, pay history), and a guided launch that makes a coin "team-run by construction" |
| How it makes money (as submitted) | Launch fee, a fee-split slot or payday fee, and a later API [S1] |
| Token | None proposed |
